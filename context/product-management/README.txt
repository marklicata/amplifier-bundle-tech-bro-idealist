AI + Product Led Growth [SY1]README

This README provides a roadmap for understanding Product-Led Growth via a set of playbooks. These tools are designed to empower product teams to achieve greater impact with more specificity in a product-led and user-focused way. As we move into building product experiences that leverage AI, these fundamentals will make sure we have the right product foundations in place to achieve impact in a PLG way. Readers are invited to work through the playbooks linearly or choose specific playbooks based on their current need.

Guide[ML2][ML3][ML4]
Playbooks will be built out over time starting with product basics and then moving into intermediate and advanced topics.

The product basics focus on foundational product building.
* Product 101 - Ethos and User Needs
o Help product teams define the ethos of their product; the purpose, mission, and vision. And then identify user needs that the product could solve for.
* Product 102 - Growth Ideas
o Help product teams define a set of prioritized growth investments based on estimated impact.
* Product 103 - Experiment Fundamentals
o Help product teams turn a set of prioritized investments with estimated impact into well-formed experiments.
* Product 104 - Metrics
o Help product teams define everything from metrics ladders to experiment success criteria in order to ensure we’re building the right products for our users.
* Product 105 - Positioning and GTM
o Help product teams position their products and/or features and bring them to market.

Intermediate topics build on product basics with specific areas and/or scenarios. Additional playbooks will be added as-needed in the future
* Product 201 - AI Product Building
* Product 202 - AI Copilots
