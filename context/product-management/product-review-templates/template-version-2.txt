Section 3: Growth Plans
This section should be no more than 3 pages on how you’ll grow the product. Growth can be whatever you define it (core product or MS+) and how it will impact the product.

Most products use the following progression for growth.
1. First, drive growth in the product as a standalone product/business.
2. Second, leverage the larger consumer ecosystem to drive growth in the product/business
3. Third, contribute back to the ecosystem to drive growth in other products

3.1 Theses for Growth
Based on your product strategy, your MS+ strategy, the growth progression above, and finally metrics + insights, tell us about the top 1-3 theses to grow the product.

* This could be opening the top of the funnel; driving deeper engagement through magic moments or integrations with other products; improving user value by directing users to adjacent products; supporting an ecosystem lever; or something else.
* What insights (data, research, signals) support this thesis? The clearer you can be about what the data is telling you to do and the impact it could have the better.

3.2 Investments
For each thesis, tell us about 1-3 investments the team is making. Big bets or “moon shots” are encouraged! For each investment, include the following detail.
* Background: What is the title of the investment? How did you identify the investment? What insights guided the team?
* Falsifiable hypothesis: What is the falsifiable hypothesis? What would prove the hypothesis false?
* Estimated impact: What is the estimated impact to product metrics in section 1 or SLT Metrics?
* Go to market: When will this investment go to market? (end users, not insider rings)
* User experience: What does the experience or flow look like to the end user?

3.3 User experiences / flows / journeys
In the presentation, show the user journey/flow for each thesis and/or investment. What will consumers see / experience in the product?


Appendix A: pre-PMF questions
Why Now
* Provide a market analysis for your product. What market conditions exist that affect the outlook of your product? Why is “right now” the time for your product? How have your users and their behaviors changed: basic environment, needs, market trends, etc.?
* Provide a competitive analysis for your product. Who are the main competitors and why? How long have they been in the market?
* Provide a summary of user research surrounding your product. What are key insights that the team has learned and is folding into their product plans?
* What is that Total Addressable Market (TAM) and Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) for your product? And what Share of Market (SOM) do you believe the product could capture?

Product North Star
* What is the North Star or Value Prop of the product? What unmet or unfulfilled needs will it solve? Is the product or experience new to the market, or a competitor fast-follow?
* What are the key segments and audiences for your product? Not SMB or IT Admin, but a specific user with specific needs. How do their unmet or unfulfilled needs differ by device type (web vs. desktop vs. mobile vs. tablet)?
* What are the critical user journeys for these users? How will the critical user journeys achieve the value prop for your audience?
* How do these critical user journeys differ by device type (web vs. desktop vs. mobile vs. tablet)?
* What is the falsifiable hypotheses1 and product strategy to achieve the mission above? What assumptions have been made about the opportunity, solution, or GTM motion that will be validated during these early stages?
o How will you leverage Microsoft’s strengths and competitive advantages?
o What must the product be great at?
o What differentiated value will the product bring?
o What will you deliver—and not deliver—in early stages?
