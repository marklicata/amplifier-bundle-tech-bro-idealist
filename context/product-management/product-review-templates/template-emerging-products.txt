APPENDIX A: INDICATORS FOR MOVING TO THE NEXT GROWTH PHASE
We as product leaders do not decide what growth phase a product is in. We discern what phase a product is in based on how fast it is growing and how much of the market it has penetrated. All products start in the incubation phase. Here are some indicators that suggest the product is healthy and moving onto product-market fit (growth phase)
1. Cohort retention is strong compared to similar products in the space that have similar levels of penetration.
2. There is a cohort of users that absolutely love the product, which is reflected in very high retention for that group.

Once in the growth phase, here are indicators that suggest the product has found product-market fit and is ready to achieve scale in the hypergrowth p hase.
1. Older cohorts’ retention curves flatten and perhaps slightly improve.
2. New users account for a decreasing percentage of total users.
3. Overall churn remains higher than resurrection but is driven primarily by the newest cohorts.
4. Engagement metrics get stronger.

More details on these phases and the criteria for moving from one to the next can be found starting on page 5 of the Consumer Fundamentals Handbook.
