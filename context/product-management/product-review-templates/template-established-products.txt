GOALS FOR P365
* What goals do you have for your P365 session? What would a successful review look like?
PRODUCT OVERVIEW / BACKGROUND
* What is the North Star or Value Prop of the product? What unmet or unfulfilled needs will it solve?
* What unique, differentiated value does the product bring? Avoid vague, visionary statements; be specific!
* What are the key segments and audiences for your product? Not SMB or IT Admin, but a specific user with specific needs. How do their unmet or unfulfilled needs differ by device type (web vs. desktop vs. mobile vs. tablet)?
* What are the critical user journeys for these users? How will the critical user journeys achieve the value prop for your audience? What funnels+metrics tell you that users are completing these journeys successfully?
* How do these critical user journeys differ by device type (web vs. desktop vs. mobile vs. tablet)?
* What user actions are associated with users being engaged in your product?
* What is the falsifiable hypotheses1 and product strategy to achieve the north star above? How will you leverage Microsoft’s strengths and competitive advantages?
SINCE THE LAST REVIEW (IF APPLICABLE)
* Provide a summary of key deliverables and impact that were shared in the last review.
* What falsifiable hypothesis2 did you think would work and did, and what did you think would work and didn’t? What was learned that was not known in the last review?
* Highlight any important changes to the falsifiable hypothesis, product vision, and go-to-market plans.
MARKET ANALYSIS
* Provide a competitive analysis for your product. Who are the main competitors and why? How does your product and performance compare? What is the market share for your product and how has it changed?
* Provide a market analysis for your product. What market conditions exist that affect the outlook of your product? How have your users and their behaviors changed: basic environment, needs, market trends, etc.?
* Provide a summary of user research surrounding your product. What are key insights that the team has learned and is folding into their product plans?
