Thesis for Growth
Based on your ethos, metrics, and insights tell us about the top 1-3 theses for growth in the product.
* This could be new user growth through virality, deeper engagement through onboarding, better retention through gamification, or something else.
* What insights (data, research, signals) support this thesis? Teams that can say "the data is telling us to do this, not that" have been celebrated.

Section 3: Growth investments
This section should be no more than 2 pages.

For each thesis, tell us about 1-3 investments the team is making. Big bets or “moon shots” are encouraged. Teams have been criticized for doing smaller experiments that don’t appear to be big enough swings. For each investment, include the following detail.
* Background: What is the title of the investment? How did you identify the investment? What data or insights guided the team?
* Falsifiable hypothesis: What is the falsifiable hypothesis? What would prove the hypothesis false?
* Estimated impact: What is the estimated impact to product metrics in section 1 or SLT Metrics?
* Go to market: When will this investment go to market? (end users, not insider rings)
* User experience: What does the experience or flow look like to the end user?

In the presentation, show the user journey/flow for each thesis and/or investment. What will consumers see / experience in the product?

Optional content:
Tell us about up to 2 important growth experiments that you ran and what you learned. If you had a previous P365 on the topic please recap previous thesis and learnings.


Section 4: SLT Asks
