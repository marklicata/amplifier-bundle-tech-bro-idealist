# Part 1: Go-to-Market Basics

A product go-to-market plan (GTM) details how we will introduce a new
product or feature to its target audience. It requires a thorough
understanding of the target audience, the competition, and market
trends. While the specific elements of a GTM plan may vary depending on
the product, the market, and the product's north star, the following are
some of the basic elements that should be included in a typical GTM
plan: 

 

## Market analysis 

Outline the target market for the product or feature including its size,
demographics, needs, and behaviors. Calculate the Total Addressable
Market (TAM) and Serviceable Available Market (SAM) for an early
indicator of opportunity size. Leverage this
[eBook](https://microsoft.sharepoint-df.com/:b:/t/AnytimeAcademy-Freelancers/EV-8SmRDFYhLsYeopeJ9LLsBlcOLAeujWQcCo5TddS0L6w?e=iNDvh8)
and
[video](https://microsoft.sharepoint-df.com/:v:/t/AnytimeAcademy-Freelancers/Ee28jyGrqyRIimOFEHtlqo4BjKU5-TFA2Vpz-0aEjsdZhw?e=AnSH6U)
for help calculating your TAM.

> ***Exploratory question:** is there a target market for this product
> or experience? What markets will rollout in what order? Based on what
> criteria?* 
>
> ***Exploratory question:** what are the growth trends or other key
> dynamics in this market? Will any of these trends accelerate or
> suppress your product's growth? *

## Customer segments 

After the target market has been defined, we can break that down in a
set of target audiences within that market. For commercial scenarios,
it's important to outline the [Decision-Making
Unit](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/:p:/t/IncubationFrameworkv-team/ES629-17Y5tEmH_L1wibgg8BDyaHG-MUPpTr3d2t4GnD-Q?e=fw5qxl&wdLOR=c9499A162-8A49-456C-B791-DB0FFDD26D67)
(DMU) or groups within the commercial customer who need to understand
the product's value prop

A few key groups to think about as you define target audiences.

- **Core users** who will actually use the product or service. And
  eventually pay for it assuming they find value.

- **Influencers** who will write and advocate for your product or
  service; usually via social media. These users are essential to
  user-generated growth.

- For commercial scenarios, **IT Admins** or "**Economic Buyers**" at
  the company who decide what products to purchase.

 

> ***Exploratory question:** What is your customer segmentation
> strategy? What segments are primary, secondary, etc.? Trying to solve
> for all segments will likely result in serving none of them.*

## Value prop and positioning 

Define how the product will be positioned in the market and how it will
be differentiated from the competition. This includes developing a brand
identity, a value proposition, and a messaging strategy that resonates
with the target audience. Focus on how the product or feature meets a
user's need. Clearly define which customer problems your product or
feature is helping to solve. [This
template](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/:w:/t/IncubationFrameworkv-team/EfaRkefaYN5Joxuoo7DUgKgB_pvgY8XBEcXD9ySRtDsXpw?e=j0shhi)
is a helpful exercise for defining your value proposition and connecting
it to different audiences.

 

> ***Exploratory question:** what is the value proposition of the
> product/experience? What is the position and messaging strategy?* 
>
> ***Exploratory question:** how do users extract value from this
> product?*
>
> ***Exploratory question:** for commercial industries, how does the
> value of the product vary across customer segments? Is this more
> valuable to one industry over another?  *

## Marketing strategy 

This strategy outlines the tactics that will be used to reach and engage
with the target audience. This includes selecting the appropriate
marketing channels (ex. social media, paid advertising, leveraging 1P
base, etc), developing content (ex. blog posts, videos, infographics),
and establishing a budget. 

 

> ***Exploratory question:** how do you plan to ensure users see & use
> your product or feature? What low CAC methods will you use to get
> users into the critical user journeys defined in the [Growth
> Ideas](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/teams/AIMomentWorkingGroup/Shared%20Documents/General/AI%20Product%20Readiness_Excellence/Playbooks/Product%20102%20-%20Growth%20Ideas.docx)
> playbook?* 
>
> ***Exploratory question:** What costs will we incur to take the
> product to market? Is the team leveraging high-cost channels (higher
> CAC)? Are you planning to run some sort of promotion?* 

## Monetization plan 

The [Ethos and User
Needs](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/:w:/t/AIMomentWorkingGroup/EaWVvYP3d3tGqMkknr_RxxEBakk9ADTZVSKxuawUEGTEng)
playbook touches on building a monetization for your product or feature.
But now that it is landing in market, we need to define HOW it will be
sold; i.e. what channels and pricing strategy will be used.

> ***Exploratory question:** What pricing strategy will you use for this
> product or feature? What channels do you plan to use to drive
> awareness or acquisition? Are they high or low cost?*

## Launch plan 

Outline the timeline and specific activities that will be carried out
during the product launch. This includes developing launch materials
(e.g., press releases, product demos, sales collateral), conducting
market research and customer validation, and preparing the sales and
marketing teams for the launch. Product launch is when end-users will
see the experience, **not insider rings**. 

**If your launch requires setting up a meter and collecting revenue**
you will need to go through the launch readiness team. This will require
owners from 5 different Microsoft teams: Business Planning, Product
Marketing, Revenue Finance, COGS Finance, and Release Ops. The product
team is responsible for providing market research (market landscape,
compete analysis, customer segments, use cases, and buyer
considerations) and the product strategy (service definition,
positioning, and roadmap). **This process can take several months.**
Connect with Business Planning and Finance early to establish the
perquisites.  

 

## Measurement 

Define the key performance indicators (KPIs) that will be used to
measure the success of the GTM plan. This includes tracking metrics such
users and user growth, new user engagement and retention, sales/revenue,
CAC, LTV, market share, and others. 

 

> ***Exploratory question**: how will you measure the success of your
> GTM motion? Leverage the
> [Metrics](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/teams/AIMomentWorkingGroup/Shared%20Documents/General/AI%20Product%20Readiness_Excellence/Playbooks/Product%20104%20-%20Metrics.docx?web=1)
> playbook where needed.*
>
> ***Exploratory question**: how will the team continuously learn and
> iterate on their GTM plan? Leverage the [Experiment
> Fundamentals](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/teams/AIMomentWorkingGroup/Shared%20Documents/General/AI%20Product%20Readiness_Excellence/Playbooks/Product%20103%20-%20Experiment%20Fundamentals.docx)
> playbook where needed.*
